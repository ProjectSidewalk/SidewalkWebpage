Training examples #

Labels the crowd voted wrong, shown to validators as common mistakes: one per mission, and anyone who doesn't disagree is told why it's wrong. A validator is done with a reason once they get an example of it right, or after @GoldExampleTable.AnswersPerReason answers on it either way. Their answer never counts as a vote, and a pinned label is never served as an ordinary label.

To add one, open a label at /admin/label/<id>. One whose imagery is gone is marked here so it can be replaced.
