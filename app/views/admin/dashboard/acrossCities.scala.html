Engagement funnels #

How many visitors reach each step, per city. The mapping funnel follows Explore onboarding (tutorial, first label, first mission); the contribution funnel asks whether they labeled or validated anything and finished a mission. Bars are scaled to visitors (100%); each step's percentage is its share of the step before.

Anonymous visitors are counted per browser (a new id whenever cookies are cleared), so the top of each funnel counts sessions, not people, and overall conversion reads low. Explore is desktop-only: mobile visitors are redirected to a landing page, so mobile drops off early by design. “Took a step” means walking some distance in a non-tutorial mission.
