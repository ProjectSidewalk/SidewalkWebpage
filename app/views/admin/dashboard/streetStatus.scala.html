Street status #

Which streets are actually available for auditing, and where are they unavailable? Every street carries a status: open (usable), no imagery (no street-view available), closed (in a region not yet opened to the public), or disabled (manually hidden, e.g. an OSM miscategorization). Use this to spot neighborhoods with lots of missing imagery or disabled streets.

Panoramas losing and regaining imagery

Each bar counts panoramas that crossed in that week and stays where it is: a panorama that went away in one week and came back in a later one is counted once in each, rather than being erased from the first. Imagery is found gone by a provider check, and found back either by a later check or by a labeler loading the panorama. A week of losses that the following weeks hand back was a provider hiccup; losses with no recoveries after them are imagery that is really gone.
