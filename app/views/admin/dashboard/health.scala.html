Health #

Database problems the app log doesn't show, across all cities, refreshed automatically. Nightly jobs and pano downloads cover this city only.

Nightly jobs #

This city's overnight jobs and their last recorded runs. An overdue, failed, or never-run job has stopped feeding whatever depends on it. Times are Pacific, before this city's scheduling offset.

Blocking locks #

Sessions blocking others from acquiring a lock, worst first. An idle session holding a strong lock (e.g. on street_edge) stalls every reader of that table.

Long-running queries #

Client queries running a long time, oldest first. A runaway query can saturate the database without blocking anyone, so it won't appear under locks.

Idle transactions #

Idle sessions holding an open transaction, oldest first. A transaction that never commits or rolls back holds its locks the whole time.

Stuck evolutions #

Play evolutions, in any city schema, that are mid-apply or recorded a problem. A failed evolution keeps that city's app from starting cleanly.

Table bloat & autovacuum #

Dead-tuple bloat and last-vacuum age for the largest tables (street_edge, label, audit_task_interaction, validation_task_interaction) across cities. High bloat with an old last vacuum means autovacuum is falling behind, often because it can't get a lock.

DB connections #

Client connections by role and state. Each city's app connects as its own role with its own pool limit; a role near its limit is under pressure.

Pano downloads #

How many of this city's labeled panos have a local backup. A labeled pano whose source has expired and has no backup can no longer be shown.
