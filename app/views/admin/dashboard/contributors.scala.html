Engagement funnels #

Of the people who arrive, how many reach each step? The mapping funnel follows Explore onboarding (tutorial → first label → first mission); the contribution funnel asks whether they labeled or validated anything and finished a mission. Bars are scaled to visitors (100%); each step's percentage is its share of the step before.

Anonymous visitors are counted per browser (a new id each time cookies are cleared), so the top of each funnel counts sessions, not people, and conversion reads low. Mobile visitors can't map: they are redirected to a landing page, so mobile drops off early by design. “Took a step” means walking some distance in a non-tutorial mission.
