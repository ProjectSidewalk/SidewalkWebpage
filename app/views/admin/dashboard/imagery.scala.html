Imagery #

When street-view imagery is re-captured, audits on the old imagery may describe features that no longer exist. Each night a rotating batch of streets is polled for capture dates, audits older than the imagery are flagged, and street priority is recalculated so Explore sends labelers back to those streets, after unaudited ones. Below: the resulting priority ranking, and whether the nightly jobs are running.

Re-audit frontier #

Every street Explore can route to, colored by priority tier. Click a segment to pin its region here and in the tables below.

The tiers are ranges of a single priority number; higher is served sooner:

priority = 1 / (1 + current audits + ½ if any audit is on replaced imagery + ¼ per low-quality audit)

Top to bottom, the tiers mean a street has current-imagery audits: never, not anymore, one, several.

Region ranking #

Explore picks a region at random from the five with the highest mean priority, then a random street among that region's top-priority ties. The tier columns count streets and sum to Streets. Click a row to highlight its streets on the map.

Next streets #

The highest-priority streets and the audit counts behind each value. Pin a region to see only its queue.

Is the pipeline alive? #

Re-audit flags come only from the nightly imagery-age poll, so a stopped poll (expired API key, rate limit, unsupported provider) looks like a city whose imagery never changes. These are the jobs' recorded runs, so a poll that stopped or polled nothing shows up here.

Streets polled per night

The dashed line is the nightly batch size; on a healthy night polled plus skipped reaches it unless fewer streets are eligible. Polled: the street got a conclusive answer and its imagery dates were saved. Skipped: at least one of its three sample points was inconclusive (bad API key, timeout, unexpected response), so it is retried the next night. A confirmed "no imagery here" counts as polled. On a dev machine, dummy API keys make every street skip.

Audits flagged and unflagged per night

An audit is flagged when at least half its street's sample points show newer imagery, and unflagged if a later poll reverses that. A long run of zeros while the poll is running is not normal.

Rotation and freshness #

The poll covers a fixed number of streets a night, audited streets first, so the time to complete a rotation bounds how stale a re-audit flag can be. A street never polled is unmeasured, not current, and is counted separately.

How stale our labels are

Streets whose imagery is newer than their latest audit, so their labels may describe features that are gone. Each bar is work waiting for a labeler; further right means the labels have been out of date longer. Streets with current audits are not plotted; they are the "audits still current" row above.
