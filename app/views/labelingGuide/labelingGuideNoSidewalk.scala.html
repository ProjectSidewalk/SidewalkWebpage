@import models.user.SidewalkUserWithRole @import play.api.Configuration @import service.CommonPageData @(commonData: CommonPageData, title: String, user: Option[SidewalkUserWithRole] )(implicit request: RequestHeader, messages: Messages, assets: AssetsFinder, config: Configuration) @layout(commonData, title, user, "no-sidewalk") {

No Sidewalk

A No Sidewalk label should be placed if there is a missing sidewalk where there should be one.

@_question("no-sidewalk-label") {Where and how should I place a No Sidewalk label?}

@Html(Messages("labeling.guide.no.sidewalk.summary"))

You should not place No Sidewalk labels in narrow side streets or alleys.

@_question("no-sidewalk-label-median") {Do I place a No Sidewalk label on a median?}

You should not place No Sidewalk labels on a median that separates the two directions of traffic, unless the median has a sidewalk that abruptly ends.
